# 🔗 Supports

They do nothing alone: they boost the gems in the sockets next to them.

**Goes on:** any item, next to a gem. Folder: `gems/supports/`.

:::info
A support gem does nothing alone. It boosts the gems in the sockets **right next to it**. See [Supports and runewords](../player-guide/supports-and-runewords.md).
:::

The 4 icons are the 4 tiers of the gem: Chipped, Flawless, Perfect, Radiant.

### Empower Support

![The 4 tiers of this gem](https://media.ssomar.com/m/docs-img-eg-strip-empower.png)

Linked gems act +2 levels higher *(level 5)*

| | Level 1 | Level 5 |
|---|---|---|
| Extra levels | 1 | 2 |
| Socketing success (%) | 85 | 65 |

**Goes on:** weapons, armour, tools, bows and crossbows, ExecutableItems items · **Trigger:** Linked gems · **If the socketing fails:** the gem shatters · **Loot weight:** 10 · **Grows with XP:** no

`/eg give <player> empower 5`
