# 📖 Enchantments

Real Minecraft enchantments whose effects are gem activators.

**Goes on:** enchanting table, anvil, books. Folder: `gems/enchantments/`.

:::info
These are real Minecraft enchantments: enchanting table, books, anvil, grindstone, villagers and loot. They are not socketed. See [Native enchantments](../questions-or-guides/tutorials/native-enchantments.md).
:::

### Aftershock

Critical hits shake the ground around you *(level 3)*

**Goes on:** axes · **Trigger:** Hit · **Grows with XP:** no

Enchantment id: `executablegems:aftershock`. Max level 3.

### Soul Reaper

Kills tear the soul out of the target *(level 3)*

**Goes on:** swords · **Trigger:** Kill · **Grows with XP:** no

Enchantment id: `executablegems:soul_reaper`. Max level 3.
