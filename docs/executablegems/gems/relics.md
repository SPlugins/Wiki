# 👑 Relics

Legendary gems torn from the bosses of the world. One relic per item.

**Goes on:** weapons (one per item). Folder: `gems/relics/`.

![Heart of the Warden firing a Sonic Boom](https://media.ssomar.com/m/docs-img-eg-gem-warden.jpg)

:::info
An item can hold only one relic. Relics are found only at Perfect or Radiant tier.
:::

The 4 icons are the 4 tiers of the gem: Chipped, Flawless, Perfect, Radiant.

### Dragon's Eye

![The 4 tiers of this gem](https://media.ssomar.com/m/docs-img-eg-strip-dragon-eye.png)

Right-click: Dragon Breath, a cone of violet fire: 16 damage and Weakness to all in front (9s) Kills grant 8s of Strength *(level 5)*

> *It blinks when no one is looking.*

| | Level 1 | Level 5 |
|---|---|---|
| Damage | 8 | 16 |
| Cooldown (s) | 21 | 9 |
| Duration (s) | 4 | 8 |
| Socketing success (%) | 75 | 55 |

**Goes on:** weapons, ExecutableItems items · **Trigger:** Right-click, Kill · **If the socketing fails:** the gem slips out · **Loot weight:** 1 (found from tier 3) · **Exclusive group:** `relic`, `right-click`

`/eg give <player> dragon_eye 5`

### Fallen Star

![The 4 tiers of this gem](https://media.ssomar.com/m/docs-img-eg-strip-star-shard.png)

Kills call a falling star on the nearest enemy: 16 damage around the impact (5s) *(level 5)*

> *It fell for a thousand years to find your hand.*

| | Level 1 | Level 5 |
|---|---|---|
| Damage | 8 | 16 |
| Cooldown (s) | 9 | 5 |
| Socketing success (%) | 75 | 55 |

**Goes on:** weapons, bows and crossbows, ExecutableItems items · **Trigger:** Kill · **If the socketing fails:** the gem slips out · **Loot weight:** 1 (found from tier 3) · **Exclusive group:** `relic`

`/eg give <player> star_shard 5`

### Heart of the Warden

![The 4 tiers of this gem](https://media.ssomar.com/m/docs-img-eg-strip-warden-heart.png)

Sneak + right-click: Sonic Boom, a shriek that tears through everything in a line: 20 damage, ignores armour (15s) *(level 5)*

> *It still beats, somewhere far below.*

| | Level 1 | Level 5 |
|---|---|---|
| Damage | 12 | 20 |
| Cooldown (s) | 35 | 15 |
| Socketing success (%) | 75 | 55 |

**Goes on:** weapons, ExecutableItems items · **Trigger:** Sneak + right-click · **If the socketing fails:** the gem slips out · **Loot weight:** 1 (found from tier 3) · **Exclusive group:** `relic`, `sneak-right-click`

`/eg give <player> warden_heart 5`
