# 🗺️ Finding gems

A gem on the ground raises a **column of light** in its colour. The higher the tier, the taller the column.

![Columns of light over dropped gems at night](</img/executablegems/loot-beams.jpg>)

| Source | Default chance | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Monsters you kill | 1% per kill | +0.5% per level of Looting |
| Monsters you kill | 3% per kill | An Unidentified Gem |
| Wither, Ender Dragon | 100%, 2 rolls | Better tiers |
| Warden | 60%, 2 rolls | Better tiers |
| Elder Guardian | 50%, 2 rolls | Better tiers |
| Structure chests | 6% to 40% | Ancient cities, bastions and end cities have the best ones |
| The [jeweller](the-jeweller.md) | Shop | Unidentified Gems |
| [Geode Gem](../gems/prospecting.md) | Mining stone | Can unearth Unidentified Gems |

These are the default values. Your server may have changed them.

**Relics** are the rarest gems. They are only found at Perfect or Radiant tier.
